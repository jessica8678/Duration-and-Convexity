2Why a waiting time predicts a price move

A yield rise makes every division slightly harsher. A payment only feels that harshness once per division it endures — so waiting time is leverage:

4Where 73.6287 comes from

Duration weighted each payment by year. Acceleration weights by year × (year + ½) — far payments dominate, because their sensitivity changes fastest.

What this leaves out
  • One flat yield for every payment, rather than a real yield curve
  • Settlement sits on a coupon date, so there is no accrued interest
  • No credit risk, no tax, no calls or prepayment
  • Yields move in parallel
